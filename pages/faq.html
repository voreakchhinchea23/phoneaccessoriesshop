Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our products, shipping, and policies.

For customers in Phnom Penh, delivery typically takes 1 to 2 business days. For provinces outside of Phnom Penh, delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days via our trusted shipping partners (e.g., Virak Buntham, J&T Express).

We accept ABA KHQR (scan to pay), Cash on Delivery (COD) for Phnom Penh, and bank transfers to our official ABA account. Please always include your name or order number in the transfer note when paying via KHQR.

Yes! Most of our electronic accessories (like chargers, power banks, and audio devices) come with a 6 to 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Phone cases and screen protectors are checked for quality before shipping but are not covered under warranty once applied or used.

If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the product and packaging. We will arrange a free replacement or a full refund. Items must be unused and in their original packaging to be eligible for a return.

Each product page clearly lists the compatible phone models (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro, Samsung Galaxy S24). If you are unsure, please use the Live Chat or Contact Us page to message us with your exact phone model, and we will confirm compatibility for you.
